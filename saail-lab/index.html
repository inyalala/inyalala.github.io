About SAAIL Lab

Our Mission

The SAAIL Lab is dedicated to pioneering transformative, ethical, and contextually-aware Artificial Intelligence solutions that leverage local strengths and collaborations. We focus on revolutionizing Sustainable Agriculture through AI, enhancing food security, and improving farmer livelihoods in East Africa.

Our Vision

To become a leading research hub in the region, conducting world-class research, developing locally attuned innovations, cultivating future AI leaders, and forging impactful strategic partnerships with institutions like the Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI (WSAI) at IIT Madras.

Founding Principal Investigator (Prospective)

Dr. Innocent Nyalala

As the prospective Founding Principal Investigator, Dr. Nyalala is establishing the SAAIL Lab at IIT Madras Zanzibar with a vision to create transformative AI solutions for sustainable agriculture and intelligent livelihoods in East Africa. The lab has been actively conducting research since October 2024.

Current Projects

Active research projects and initiatives driving innovation in AI for agriculture and sustainable livelihoods.

🌿

Deep Learning for Fine-Grained Quality Grading of Zanzibar Cloves

Agriculture AI MTech Project

Leading MTech project with Mr. Patrick Ndowo focusing on precision crop management through advanced deep learning techniques for quality assessment of Zanzibar's premium clove production.

Student: Mr. Patrick Ndowo
Focus: Precision Crop Management
Status: Active Research
🏥

Placental Weight & Volume Estimation from 2D Ultrasound

Healthcare AI MTech Project

AI-powered medical image analysis for neonatal outcome prediction, part of CPHK collaboration (Pemba) and forthcoming work with PlacentaVision (Penn State University).

Student: Ms. Twamaa Makame
Collaboration: Penn State University
Impact: 12 Research Articles Prepared
👁️

E-Vision: Intelligent Assistive Application for Visually Impaired

Accessibility MTech Project

Developing low-cost, low-latency edge AI solutions for assistive technologies, in collaboration with Cadre Technologies to enhance accessibility for the visually impaired.

Student: Mr. Shivam Vyas
Partner: Cadre Technologies
Mentor: Muneer Khan
📊

Agricultural Data Ecosystems Development

Data Science Collaboration

Collaborating with RuTAG (IIT Madras Chennai) on comprehensive dataset creation for AI applications in agriculture, focusing on cardamom, millets, and walnuts.

Partner: RuTAG IIT Madras
Crops: Cardamom, Millets, Walnuts
Type: Dataset Creation
🔗

Blockchain for Zanzibar Spice Supply Chain

Blockchain Supply Chain

Exploring blockchain technology to enhance traceability and transparency in Zanzibar's valuable spice supply chain, ensuring quality and authenticity.

Focus: Supply Chain Traceability
Region: Zanzibar
Status: Exploration Phase

AI-Driven Air Quality Index Monitoring

Environmental AI IoT
