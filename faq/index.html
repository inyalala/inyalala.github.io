Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions I get most often — read this before reaching out, it may save us both time.

Are you currently accepting new MTech or PhD students?

This varies by semester and available funding. See the Work With Me page for current openings, or email me directly with your CV and a short statement of research interest.

Can you write me a recommendation letter?

I'm generally happy to write letters for students I have directly supervised or taught, provided there is sufficient notice (at least 3–4 weeks) and you share your CV, the program/opportunity details, and a draft of your own statement.

I'd like to collaborate on a research project — how should I reach out?

Email me directly with a short (2–3 paragraph) description of the proposed collaboration, why it fits SAAIL Lab's research areas (AI for agriculture, healthcare AI, responsible AI, Swahili NLP), and your own background. See Work With Me for more detail.

Are you available for speaking engagements, keynotes, or panels?

Yes, particularly on AI for agriculture, responsible/ethical AI in the African context, and AI education. Please email with the event details, date, format (in-person/virtual), and expected audience. A short bio and headshot for your own use are available on the Media Kit page.

I'm a journalist/blogger — can I get a bio and photo?

Yes — the Media Kit page has a downloadable headshot, short and long bio, and institutional logos ready to use.

Do you review manuscripts, or can I ask you to review my paper?

I review for journals and conferences within my area of expertise (AI for agriculture, healthcare AI, responsible AI) through the standard editorial process. I'm not able to informally review manuscripts outside that process due to time constraints.

What is the best way to contact you?

Email is best — see the Contact section on the homepage for both institutional and personal addresses, office hours, and phone numbers. Please include enough context in your first message (who you are, what you need) so I can respond usefully.
