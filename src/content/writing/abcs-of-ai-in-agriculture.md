Artificial intelligence in agriculture sits at the intersection of soil physics, botany, statistics, embedded hardware, and human livelihoods. Students entering from computer science rarely see why a dust storm or midday tropical shadow breaks an ImageNet classifier; agronomy and animal science scholars rarely hear why random dataset splits overstate accuracy on smallholder crops.

I assembled this illustrated **A-to-Z primer** for high school students, undergraduates, graduate fellows, and postdocs exploring agricultural AI, and as conceptual scaffolding toward an upcoming Agricultural AI elective at IIT Madras Zanzibar. Every single letter pairs a clear plain-English explanation, current state of the art, open problems, and direct links whenever the topic connects to peer-reviewed SAAIL Lab and collaborative studies across **tomato vision**, **cassava pathology**, **Zanzibar cloves**, **poultry carcasses**, **tillage physics**, **Swahili dialects**, and **African AI benchmarks**.
