This short guide answers common logistics questions for academic collaborators, visiting seminar speakers, external thesis examiners, and prospective students traveling to **IIT Madras Zanzibar** (IITMZ), India's first international IIT campus located on Unguja Island, Tanzania.

## 1. Airport, Ferry & Visa Basics

- **International Arrival (ZNZ)**: Fly into **Abeid Amani Karume International Airport (ZNZ)**. Direct and connecting flights operate regularly from Dar es Salaam (DAR), Nairobi (NBO), Kilimanjaro (JRO), Addis Ababa (ADD), Doha (DOH), Dubai (DXB), and Muscat (MCT).
- **Dar es Salaam Ferry Option**: Fast passenger ferries (Azam Marine / Kilimanjaro) cross from Dar es Salaam port to Malindi Ferry Terminal in Stone Town in roughly 90 to 110 minutes.
- **Entry Requirements**: Check Tanzania e-Visa or visa-on-arrival rules for your passport well ahead of travel, and keep your host invitation letter printed or offline on your phone. Most international travelers also carry a Yellow Fever certificate when arriving from or transiting through endemic countries.

## 2. Reaching the Bweleo Campus

The permanent IIT Madras Zanzibar campus sits in **Bweleo** along the southwestern coast of Unguja, approximately 25 to 35 minutes south of Stone Town and about 20 minutes from ZNZ airport depending on traffic.

- **Campus Coordinates**: `-6.29556, 39.28768` (Bweleo, P.O. Box 2496, Zanzibar, Tanzania).
- **Local Timezone**: East Africa Time (**EAT, UTC+3**), observed year-round with no daylight saving shifts.
- **On-Campus Office**: My office is **Room 105 (F1)** in the School of Engineering and Science. Regularly scheduled student office hours run Wednesdays, 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM EAT; visiting researchers can coordinate an appointment ahead of arrival.

## 3. Accommodation Areas & Everyday Essentials

- **Where Visitors Stay**: Visiting speakers and academic partners generally choose either **Stone Town** (historic walkability, ferries, wide selection of guesthouses, ~30 minutes by taxi to Bweleo) or **Fumba Town / Fumba Peninsula** (newer residential developments and quiet coastal stays closer to Bweleo, roughly 10 to 15 minutes from campus).
- **Connectivity & Power**: Local SIM cards (Zantel / Yas / Airtel / Vodacom Tanzania) with LTE data bundles are straightforward to purchase at ZNZ arrivals with a passport. Tanzania uses **Type D and Type G** (UK 3-pin rectangular) electrical sockets at 230V / 50Hz.
- **Currency**: Tanzanian Shilling (TZS), with card acceptance at major hotels and cash/mobile money helpful for small shops and taxis.

## 4. Coordinating Seminars, Collaborations & Lab Visits

To schedule a research seminar in the School of Engineering and Science, discuss an IC&SR/industry project, or visit SAAIL Lab in person, write in advance to **innocent@iitmz.ac.in** with your arrival dates and proposed topic. You can also review our [collaboration guide](/work-with-me/) or inspect active coursework on the [Teaching Portal](/courses/).
