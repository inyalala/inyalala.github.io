The SAAIL Lab

Sustainable AI for Agriculture & Intelligent Livelihoods

Based at IIT Madras Zanzibar, the SAAIL Lab is a hub for transformative innovation. We bridge the gap between high-end computational research and ground-level implementation to empower communities across East Africa and beyond.

Mission

Harnessing AI to uplift rural livelihoods through technology.

Innovation

Designing region-specific neural architectures and datasets.
